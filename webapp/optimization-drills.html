Find all critical points of z = f(x,y), and classify each one (local minimum, local maximum or saddle point) using the Hessian criterion.

Answer

Step-by-Step Solution

Free extrema cover quadratic forms, separable and coupled polynomial systems, and exponential/logarithmic functions, classified via the first and second principal minors of the Hessian. Constrained extrema cover linear, product and distance objectives on circles, ellipses, hyperbolas, lines, planes and spheres (including true three-variable problems), solved by Lagrange multipliers and classified by comparing candidate values on a compact constraint set, or by a boundedness argument; a few are classic applied problems (maximum-volume box, maximum-area rectangle). Every exercise is built to have a small number of non-degenerate critical points.
